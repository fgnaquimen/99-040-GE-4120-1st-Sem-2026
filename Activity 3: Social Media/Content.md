Working on the cyberbullying infographic showed me how to turn heavy research from sources like UNICEF, UNESCO, and OECD into a clear visual story about online harassment and how tech can help stop it. I practiced pairing short summaries with custom icons, numbered solution cards, and a full reference list. Trying to fit statistics, four tech-based solutions, and practical safety tips onto one long graphic without overwhelming the reader was the hardest part, so I broke the content up using color-coded blocks and short bullet points. These skills will be useful when I need to create patient education materials or public health campaigns, though I definitely need to proofread large display titles more carefully so I catch typos like the extra "L" in "CYBERBULLLYING".
