Putting together our Group 5 presentation on Google Docs taught me how helpful features like suggesting mode, version history, and document tabs are, especially when comparing the platform to Microsoft Word. I also picked up a fun design trick by styling our slides to look like an actual Google Docs workspace and kicking things off with a "Live Google Docs Race" to get the class involved. Dividing the workload among nine members without dragging out the report was a bit tricky, so we organized our flow into a quick class game, a focused discussion, and a live demo with a QR code. For my upcoming nursing papers and study guides, I plan to rely more on these shared tools to keep group edits organized, and next time I will proofread our slide headers more carefully so typos like "GOOGLE DOGS" do not sneak in.
